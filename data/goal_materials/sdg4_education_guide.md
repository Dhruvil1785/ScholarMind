# SDG 4: Quality Education — Domain Knowledge Guide

## Goal Overview
Sustainable Development Goal 4 (SDG 4) aims to "ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all." Education is a foundational human right and an indispensable driver for sustainable development, poverty eradication, gender equality, and economic prosperity.

## Key Targets of SDG 4
1. **Target 4.1: Free, Equitable, and Quality Primary and Secondary Education**
   - Ensure all boys and girls complete free, equitable, and quality primary and secondary education leading to relevant and effective learning outcomes.
2. **Target 4.2: Early Childhood Development and Pre-Primary Education**
   - Ensure all girls and boys have access to quality early childhood development, care, and pre-primary education so they are ready for primary education.
3. **Target 4.3: Equal Access to Technical, Vocational, and Higher Education**
   - Ensure equal access for all women and men to affordable and quality technical, vocational, and tertiary education, including university.
4. **Target 4.4: Relevant Skills for Decent Work**
   - Substantially increase the number of youth and adults who have relevant skills, including technical and vocational skills, for employment, decent jobs, and entrepreneurship.
5. **Target 4.5: Gender Equality and Inclusion**
   - Eliminate gender disparities in education and ensure equal access to all levels of education and vocational training for vulnerable populations, persons with disabilities, indigenous peoples, and children in vulnerable situations.
6. **Target 4.6: Universal Literacy and Numeracy**
   - Ensure that all youth and a substantial proportion of adults, both men and women, achieve literacy and numeracy.
7. **Target 4.7: Education for Sustainable Development and Global Citizenship**
   - Ensure all learners acquire knowledge and skills needed to promote sustainable development, human rights, gender equality, peace and non-violence, and global citizenship.
